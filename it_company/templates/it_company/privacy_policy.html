1. Introduction

Welcome to SCALLA. We provide professional IT solutions, technical support, network solutions, and related technology services.

When you submit a contact request, we may collect:

We may use your information to:

Providing personal information through our contact form is voluntary. However, certain details may be necessary for us to respond to your inquiry.

We do not sell personal information collected through our contact form.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain relevant business records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

If additional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you and correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
